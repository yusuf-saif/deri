The invitation

Before the music, before the aisle, there is a promise.

We invite you into more than a date on a calendar. This is the story of two lives being joined before God, welcomed by family, and celebrated by everyone who has prayed, laughed, waited, and walked with us.

Every gathering has its own meaning. The traditional wedding honours where we come from. The court wedding speaks to covenant and commitment. The white wedding brings us to the altar. Thanksgiving sends us forward with gratitude.
